  • Description

    Helga Svelgen's (Charlize Theron) bloodied costume with lingerie from John Herzfeld's 2 Days in the Valley. Helga wore her lingerie bodysuit to seduce her boyfriend Lee Woods (James Spader) in their hotel room, and later had her catsuit costume bloodied when she got into a fight with Becky Foxx (Teri Hatcher).

    This lot consists of a cream suede John David Ridge jumpsuit labeled "Ms. Charlize Theron" featuring a bullet hole and stage blood on the right hip; a pair of white leather Nathalie M strappy block heels covered in stage blood; and a torn white cotton lace lingerie bodysuit. This bodysuit was previously displayed at Planet Hollywood Resort & Casino Las Vegas Room 1686. This lot exhibits stiff material on the jumpsuit's stage blood and worn fraying material on the heels.
