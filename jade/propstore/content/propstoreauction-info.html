    National Autistic Society
    The UK National Autistic Society is a leading charity dedicated to supporting autistic people and their families. Founded in 1962, the organisation works to raise awareness, improve understanding, and champion the rights of autistic individuals across the UK. The National Autistic Society offers a wide range of services, including education, training and advocacy, whilst also campaigning for better support and inclusion in all areas of society. Through its work, the charity aims to create a society that works for autistic people.
